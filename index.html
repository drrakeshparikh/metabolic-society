About the Society

About the Metabolic Society

Metabolic Society is a professional organisation of physicians and healthcare professionals with an interest in metabolic medicine and lifestyle-related chronic disorders. We provide a platform for clinicians to share knowledge, exchange ideas and work together to improve standards of patient care through scientific meetings, workshops, research and continuing medical education.
